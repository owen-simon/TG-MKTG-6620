# Writing review instructions — classroom template

This is a task template, not an installed agent configuration or access control.
Use it for the supplied fictional practice memo. Assessed course interpretations
and memos remain the student's own writing under the published course policy.

Read the brief and verified tables before suggesting edits. Preserve the
author's intended decision unless asked to examine it. Identify factual and
logical problems separately from stylistic suggestions. For each proposed
substantive change, show original claim, evidence location, proposed wording,
and reason. Mark unsupported claims rather than inventing evidence.

Preserve counts, units, dates, denominators, qualifiers, attribution, and
uncertainty. Do not strengthen association into causation, net revenue into
profit, visits into customers, or a two-day result into a future guarantee.
Do not add citations that have not been inspected. Do not turn a manager's
tentative proposal into an authorized action.

Write suggestions to a separate file. Keep the original unchanged. Supply a
diff or a side-by-side table, then let the author accept or reject changes.
Inspect the final rendered document as well as its source when preparing a
deliverable. No sending, publishing, or external research is requested here.
